# French Algeria SEO Plan

Goal: improve visibility for French-language brand searches, especially queries that imply Algeria or Bir Khadem, without creating doorway pages or fake local signals.

## What we changed technically

- Kept `/fr/` as the canonical French URL set.
- Added both `fr` and `fr-DZ` hreflang annotations to the same French URLs.
- Kept `x-default` on `/en/`.
- Marked French HTML documents as `fr-DZ` so the rendered language tag matches the regional intent.

## Why this helps

- `hreflang` does not boost rankings by itself, but it helps Google understand that the French pages are meant for a French-speaking Algerian audience.
- `fr-DZ` is useful when the same French content is aimed at Algeria, while `fr` remains the generic language signal.
- This is a stronger regional signal than only using `fr`.

## GBP and local authority plan

- Claim or verify Google Business Profile with the exact legal/company name.
- Use one primary category: General Contractor.
- Keep the office address as Bir Khadem, Algiers, Algeria.
- Add real project photos, services, hours, website, email, and phone.
- Re-check Bing Places and Apple Business Connect so the same NAP is present there too.

## Citation plan

- Submit or clean up the company on major business platforms first.
- Then add industry and local citations that can be verified with the same NAP.
- Prefer real business directories, chamber sites, partner/vendor profiles, and local media mentions.
- Avoid low-quality mass submissions or duplicate listings.

## Backlink plan

- Ask project partners, consultants, suppliers, and subcontractors for real mentions and links.
- Publish project case studies that can be referenced by partners.
- Aim for local PR and industry coverage rather than generic link exchanges.
- Use French-language assets when targeting French search results.

## Monthly measurement

- Google Search Console: French query impressions, clicks, and average position.
- Indexing: confirm `/fr/` pages stay indexed and canonicalized correctly.
- GBP: calls, website clicks, and directions.
- Citation health: NAP consistency across all key profiles.
- Brand mentions: French-language mentions of Igloo Construction and Igloo Groupe.

## Rules to avoid

- No fake reviews.
- No keyword stuffing in brand names.
- No duplicate city pages unless there is real, unique operational proof.
- No inconsistent phone numbers, addresses, or company names.
